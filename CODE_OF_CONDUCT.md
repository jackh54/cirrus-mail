# Code of Conduct

Cirrus Mail adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

It applies to everyone taking part in the project: in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere else you represent the project.

In short: be kind, assume good faith, welcome newcomers, and keep criticism focused on the work, not the person. Harassment of any kind isn't tolerated.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through the contact details on [@jackh54's GitHub profile](https://github.com/jackh54). Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code, following the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant.
